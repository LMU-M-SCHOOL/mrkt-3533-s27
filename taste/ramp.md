# The ramp

The mix of work you see shifts across the semester. Early weeks reward work that reads in seconds. Later weeks include work that confuses on first look and rewards a second, because that is where a trained eye comes from.

| Weeks | Level | What to pick |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Calibration round 1 | The fixed calibration set, scored by everyone |
| 2 to 6 | Accessible | US or UK work, in English, one medium, reads in seconds |
| 7 | Mixed | Either level |
| 8 | Calibration round 2 | The fixed set again, plus a mixed pick |
| 9 to 10 | Mixed | Either level |
| 11 to 13 | Demanding | Non-US work, work that needs the case study to understand, an unfamiliar medium, or an integrated campaign |
| 14 | Calibration round 3 | The fixed set a third time, plus a demanding pick |
| 15 | Archive handoff | No teardown. The index is finalized and handed to the directors |
