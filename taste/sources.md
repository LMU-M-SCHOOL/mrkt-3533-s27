# Sources

Pick weekly teardown subjects from this list. Published within the last 12 months.

## Campaigns and advertising

- Ads of the World, https://www.adsoftheworld.com/campaigns. Filter by medium, industry, and region. Has a Student section and curated collections.
- Lürzer's Archive, https://www.luerzersarchive.com/. Forty years of print and film. Paywalled past a free look, so optional.
- Muse by Clios, https://musebyclios.com/. Editorial, Ad of the Week, and the reasoning behind choices.
- Communication Arts, https://www.commarts.com/. Juried annuals since 1959, with an Interactive discipline.
- Famous Campaigns, https://www.famouscampaigns.com/. PR and earned-media ideas. The idea that travels, not the craft.
- Little Black Book, https://lbbonline.com/. High volume, agency-submitted. Useful as a contrast to the curated sites.

## Landing pages and product

- Awwwards, https://www.awwwards.com/. Jury-scored on Design 40, Usability 30, Creativity 20, Content 10.
- Siteinspire, https://www.siteinspire.com/, or Land-book, https://land-book.com/. Landing pages.
- Mobbin, https://mobbin.com/. Product and app flows.

## Effectiveness

- Effie case library, https://effie.org/. Winning cases, including M-School's own where the directors share them.

## Not on the list

PSFK, Springwise, and The Cool Hunter were dropped. PSFK is now an AI foresight product, Springwise covers sustainability innovation, and The Cool Hunter has not updated its lifestyle feed since June 2026.
